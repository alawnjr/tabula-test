             LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT OF ELINOR MARGARET HAYES

I, ELINOR MARGARET HAYES, residing at 412 Riverside Drive, Apt 7C,
New York, New York 10025, being of sound mind and not acting under
duress or undue influence, do hereby make, publish and declare this
to be my Last Will and Testament, hereby revoking all wills and
codicils previously made by me.

ARTICLE I — IDENTIFICATION
    A. I was born on March 18, 1942, in Boston, Massachusetts.
    B. My husband, Robert James Hayes, predeceased me on April 9, 2017.
    C. I have two living children:
       (1) Catherine Rose Hayes-Park, born July 2, 1969, residing
           at 27 Linden Court, Brooklyn, New York 11215.
       (2) Daniel James Hayes, born February 14, 1972, residing at
           188 Pearl Street, Apt 3, Portland, Maine 04101.

ARTICLE II — DEBTS AND EXPENSES
    I direct my Executor to pay from my residuary estate all of my
    legally enforceable debts, the expenses of my last illness, and
    the costs of administering my estate.

ARTICLE III — SPECIFIC BEQUESTS
    A. I give my Steinway model B grand piano (serial no. 391-204) to
       my granddaughter, ANYA HAYES-PARK, if she survives me. The
       estimated value of this piano is forty-eight thousand dollars
       ($48,000.00).
    B. I give the sum of TEN THOUSAND DOLLARS ($10,000.00) to the
       COLUMBIA UNIVERSITY PRESS in recognition of my career.

ARTICLE IV — RESIDUARY ESTATE
    I give, devise and bequeath all the rest, residue and remainder
    of my estate, of whatever kind and wherever situated, including
    any property over which I have a power of appointment, in equal
    shares (50% / 50%) to my two children, CATHERINE ROSE HAYES-PARK
    and DANIEL JAMES HAYES, per stirpes.

ARTICLE V — APPOINTMENT OF EXECUTOR
    A. I nominate my daughter, CATHERINE ROSE HAYES-PARK, as Executor
       of this Will, to serve without bond.
    B. If she is unable or unwilling to serve, I nominate my son,
       DANIEL JAMES HAYES, as successor Executor.

ARTICLE VI — POWERS OF EXECUTOR
    My Executor shall have all powers conferred by the laws of the
    State of New York upon executors and administrators, including
    without limitation the power to sell, lease, and mortgage real
    and personal property without court order.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have signed and declared this instrument to
be my Last Will and Testament on this 12th day of November, 2018,
at New York, New York.

____________________________________
Elinor Margaret Hayes — Testatrix
